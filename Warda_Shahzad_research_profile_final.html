About

I'm a PhD candidate in Chemistry (Materials Chemistry) at COMSATS University Islamabad (Abbottabad Campus), working on the structural, optical, and electronic properties of kesterite thin films for thin-film solar cells. My research centers on alkali metal doping with lithium, sodium, and potassium, targeting suppression of Cu/Zn antisite defects and secondary phases (ZnS, Cu₂SnS₃), the primary loss mechanisms limiting CZTS device efficiency.

I synthesize films by solution-based chemical deposition and characterize them across XRD, SEM/EDX, and UV-Vis spectroscopy, using Urbach energy and dislocation density as quantitative proxies for defect suppression across each doping series. I'm currently seeking a postdoctoral position to extend this work to new synthesis platforms and semiconductor material systems.

Supervisor: Prof. Dr. Bushra Ismail (bushraismail@cuiatd.edu.pk)

Research overview

LCZTS series

Lithium doping

Tuned the bandgap from 1.36 to 1.27 eV and reduced dislocation density from 7.3 to 2.13 × 10¹⁴ m⁻², with electrical conductivity reaching 3.6 Ω⁻¹cm⁻¹ through Li doping and two-step annealing.

NCZTS series

Sodium doping

Narrowed the bandgap from 1.50 to 1.00 eV and reduced microstrain from 0.37 × 10⁻³ to 0.13 × 10⁻³, linking Na incorporation to structural and optical changes in CBD-grown CZTS.

KCZTS series

Potassium doping

Expanded the lattice (a: 5.43→5.49 Å), increased crystallite size from 40 to 52 nm, and tuned the bandgap from 1.50 to 1.42 eV with absorption exceeding 10⁴ cm⁻¹.

Across the Li–Na–K doping series: demonstrated a consistent structure–property trend in which alkali incorporation narrowed the CZTS bandgap by 0.06–0.5 eV while improving crystallinity, with dislocation density reduced by approximately 2–4× across the studied systems.
