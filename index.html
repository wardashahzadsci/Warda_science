About

I'm a PhD candidate in Chemistry at COMSATS University Islamabad (Abbottabad Campus), working on the structural, optical, and electronic properties of kesterite thin films for thin-film solar cells. My work centers on alkali metal doping — lithium, sodium, and potassium — as a route to suppressing Cu/Zn antisite defects, the primary loss mechanism limiting CZTS device efficiency.

I synthesize films by chemical bath deposition and characterize them across XRD, SEM, EDS, UV-Vis, and Raman, using Urbach energy and dislocation density as quantitative proxies for defect suppression across each doping series.

Supervisor: Dr. Bushra Ismail bushraismail@cuiatd.edu.pk

Research overview

LCZTS series

Lithium doping

Probing Li incorporation effects on crystallite growth, microstrain, and optical absorption in the CZTS lattice.

NCZTS series

Sodium doping

Na segregation at grain boundaries and its influence on carrier transport and bandgap tuning.

KCZTS series

Potassium doping

Current focus — mapping non-monotonic concentration response and its link to antisite defect suppression.
